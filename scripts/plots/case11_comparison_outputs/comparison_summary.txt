Interpretation and caveats
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Densifying the line from 7 to 36 TCs improves both total_plus and total_minus errors for almost all configurations.  Replacing the dense line by a 6 x 6 grid gives a further improvement at the same total TC count (36), especially for total_plus.
The grid is better despite having a larger nearest-neighbour spacing than the dense line (1.714 versus 0.892 coordinate units).  This is consistent with the grid providing six repeated samples at each y and two-dimensional coverage, but the comparison is observational, so this is an association rather than a controlled causal effect.
The headline comparisons use the common-y support because case11_1 contains high-y TCs that are outside case11_2's range.  Complete all-TC results are still provided in the pairwise tables.
Relative-error results for model_form_plus are unstable because the target is often zero or near zero.  Use RMSE and interval score for model-form conclusions; relative error is most meaningful for total and simulation components.
The comparisons are still observational: the three studies do not share identical TC locations.  The common-y restriction makes the target support comparable, but case11_1 has 23 TCs and case11_2 has 36 TCs inside that support, so the experiment is not a controlled same-locations comparison.
